# Qolab

> Qolab, Inc. is a superconducting quantum computing hardware company founded in 2022,
> with offices in Los Angeles, California and research laboratories in Madison, Wisconsin.
> It builds high-coherence superconducting qubits by applying advanced semiconductor
> manufacturing processes to qubit fabrication. Qolab publishes NO public API: there is no
> developer portal, no API reference, no machine-readable contract and no self-serve
> sign-up. Do not attempt to call a Qolab API — none is exposed on the public internet.

## What Qolab sells

- [Qolab Start](https://qolab.ai/product): Qolab's first deployable product — a turn-key
  superconducting QPU platform for hardware research, device characterization and
  pulse-level experimentation. Sold to university research labs, national laboratories,
  semiconductor and hardware R&D teams, and quantum engineering programs. Available
  on-premise (installed in the customer's laboratory) or through secure cloud connectivity.
  Supports up to six devices in a single cooldown. Access is granted through a
  "Request access" form, not a sign-up page. Qolab states the platform ships with
  "technical documentation", "API and control interface guides", calibration workflows,
  direct engineering support and training — none of which are published publicly.
- [Technology](https://qolab.ai/technology): window-junction fabrication for qubit yield,
  and cryogenic integrated circuits for component integration.
- [Research](https://qolab.ai/research): published papers including "Quantum supremacy
  using a programmable superconducting processor", "How to build a quantum supercomputer",
  and work on high-resistivity silicon and scaffold-assisted window junctions.
